# A2 · Lesson 1 — Greetings & Introducing Yourself

## 🎯 Goal
After this lesson you'll be able to introduce yourself confidently as a developer —
in a meeting, an interview, or when meeting a new team.

---

## 📖 Words & phrases

| English | Meaning (Hindi hint) | Example |
|---------|----------------------|---------|
| Nice to meet you | आपसे मिलकर अच्छा लगा | "Hi, nice to meet you." |
| I work as… | मैं … के रूप में काम करता हूँ | "I work as a backend developer." |
| I'm responsible for… | मैं … के लिए ज़िम्मेदार हूँ | "I'm responsible for the payment service." |
| I have X years of experience | मेरे पास X साल का अनुभव है | "I have four years of experience in Java." |
| I'm currently working on… | मैं अभी … पर काम कर रहा हूँ | "I'm currently working on a Spring Boot API." |

---

## 💬 Dialogue — Meeting a new team

> **Lead:** Hi, welcome to the team! I'm Priya, the tech lead.
> **You:** Hi Priya, nice to meet you. I'm Sanjay.
> **Lead:** Great to have you. What's your background?
> **You:** I'm a Java developer. I have around four years of experience, mostly with
> Spring Boot and REST APIs.
> **Lead:** Perfect, that's exactly what we need. What were you working on recently?
> **You:** I was working on a microservice for order processing — building the APIs
> and writing the unit tests.

---

## 🔑 Key phrases to reuse

- "Hi, I'm **[name]**. Nice to meet you."
- "I'm a **Java / backend / full-stack** developer."
- "I have **[number]** years of experience in **[tech]**."
- "I'm currently working on **[project]**."
- "My main area is **[area]**."

---

## 🗣️ Say it out loud (drill)

Say each sentence 3 times. Then fill the blanks with YOUR real details and say them aloud:

1. "Hi, I'm __________. Nice to meet you."
2. "I work as a __________ developer."
3. "I have __________ years of experience in __________."
4. "I'm currently working on __________."

**Challenge:** Give a 30-second self-introduction out loud. Record it. Listen back.

---

## ⚠️ Watch out (common Hindi-speaker mistake)

- ❌ "I am having 4 years experience."
- ✅ "I **have** 4 years **of** experience."
- **Why:** "Have" for possession is NOT used in continuous (-ing) form. And we need
  "**of**" → "years **of** experience".

Another one:
- ❌ "Myself Sanjay."
- ✅ "I'm Sanjay." / "My name is Sanjay."
- **Why:** "Myself Sanjay" is direct translation from Hindi ("मैं खुद") — it's not natural English.
