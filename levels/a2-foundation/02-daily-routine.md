# A2 · Lesson 2 — Talking About Your Work & Daily Routine

## 🎯 Goal
Describe your typical workday in English — useful for small talk, interviews
("walk me through your day"), and standups.

---

## 📖 Words & phrases

| English | Meaning (Hindi hint) | Example |
|---------|----------------------|---------|
| A typical day | एक आम दिन | "A typical day starts with a standup." |
| First / then / after that | पहले / फिर / उसके बाद | "First I check emails, then I start coding." |
| I usually… | मैं आमतौर पर… | "I usually review PRs in the morning." |
| Deal with / handle | निपटना / संभालना | "I handle bug fixes in the afternoon." |
| Wrap up | खत्म करना / समेटना | "I wrap up by updating the ticket." |

---

## 💬 Dialogue — "What does your day look like?"

> **Colleague:** So what does a typical day look like for you?
> **You:** It usually starts with our daily standup at 10. After that, I check if there
> are any pull requests to review. Then I spend most of the day coding — right now I'm
> building new REST endpoints. In the afternoon I fix bugs and write tests. Before I log
> off, I update my Jira tickets.
> **Colleague:** Sounds busy!
> **You:** Yeah, but I like it. I enjoy solving problems.

---

## 🔑 Key phrases to reuse

- "My day usually starts with **[activity]**."
- "First… then… after that… finally…"
- "I spend most of the day **[verb-ing]**."
- "Before I log off, I **[activity]**."

---

## 🗣️ Say it out loud (drill)

Describe YOUR real day in 5 sentences, out loud, using **first / then / after that / finally**:

1. "My day usually starts with __________."
2. "First, I __________."
3. "Then I __________."
4. "After that, I __________."
5. "Finally, I __________."

Record it. Did you pause too much? Say it again, smoother.

---

## ⚠️ Watch out (common Hindi-speaker mistake)

- ❌ "I am going office daily."
- ✅ "I go **to** the office **every day**." / "I go to office daily." *(both ok, "to the office" is more standard)*
- **Why:** We need the preposition "**to**", and simple present ("I go") for habits — not continuous ("I am going").

- ❌ "Daily I am doing standup."
- ✅ "I **have** a standup **every day**." / "We do a daily standup."
- **Why:** Habit = simple present. "Every day" usually goes at the end.
