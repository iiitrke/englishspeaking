# ✅ A2 Self-Check

Do these **out loud** and **record** yourself. Then listen and self-correct.
No one is judging — this is just for you.

## Speaking prompts (speak 30–60 seconds each)

1. Introduce yourself as a developer: name, role, experience, current project.
2. Describe your typical workday using *first / then / after that / finally*.
3. Pretend you just joined a new team. Say hello and tell them your background.

## Check yourself

- [ ] Did I say "I **have** X years **of** experience" (not "I am having")?
- [ ] Did I avoid "Myself Sanjay" and say "I'm Sanjay"?
- [ ] Did I use simple present for habits ("I **go**", "I **check**")?
- [ ] Could I speak for 30 seconds without a long silence?
- [ ] Did I sound calm, not rushed?

## How to self-correct

1. Listen to your recording once, just to hear the overall flow.
2. Listen again and write down 1–2 mistakes you notice.
3. Say those exact sentences again, correctly, 3 times.
4. Don't try to fix everything at once — fix one thing per day.

✅ If you can do all 3 prompts without freezing, move on to **B1**.
