# ✅ B1 Self-Check

Speak **out loud** and **record**. Then self-correct.

## Speaking prompts (speak 30–60 seconds each)

1. Give your real standup update (yesterday / today / blockers).
2. You just joined a video call with a client. Say hello, handle a small audio glitch,
   and ask them to repeat something.
3. You're stuck on a bug. Ask a senior for help politely and confirm their suggestion.
4. Order a meal at a restaurant (play both roles).

## Check yourself

- [ ] Did I use past tense for "yesterday" ("I completed", not "I am completing")?
- [ ] Did I say "I can't **hear** you" (not "listen you")?
- [ ] Did I use "for" + duration and "since" + starting point correctly?
- [ ] Did I say "explain this **to me**" and "I have a **question**"?
- [ ] Did I sound polite ("Could you…please") instead of commanding?

## How to self-correct

1. Listen once for flow, once for mistakes.
2. Fix ONE mistake type this week (e.g. just "for/since"). Don't overload yourself.
3. Re-record the same prompt tomorrow and compare — you'll hear improvement.

✅ If you can handle a standup, a call, and asking for help smoothly, move on to **B2**.
