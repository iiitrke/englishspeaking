# B1 · Lesson 2 — Phone & Video Calls

## 🎯 Goal
Join a call, handle audio problems, and ask someone to repeat — without panic.

---

## 📖 Words & phrases

| English | Meaning (Hindi hint) | Example |
|---------|----------------------|---------|
| Can you hear me? | क्या आप मुझे सुन सकते हैं? | "Hi, can you hear me okay?" |
| You're breaking up | आपकी आवाज़ कट रही है | "Sorry, you're breaking up a bit." |
| Could you repeat that? | क्या आप दोहरा सकते हैं? | "Sorry, could you repeat that?" |
| You're on mute | आप म्यूट पर हैं | "I think you're on mute." |
| Let me share my screen | मैं स्क्रीन शेयर करता हूँ | "Let me share my screen." |
| Go ahead | बोलिए / आगे बढ़िए | "Go ahead, I'm listening." |

---

## 💬 Dialogue — Joining a video call

> **You:** Hi everyone, can you hear me okay?
> **Client:** Yes, loud and clear.
> **You:** Great. Just give me a second to share my screen.
> **Client:** Sure, go ahead.
> *(audio glitch)*
> **You:** Sorry, you broke up there for a moment. Could you repeat the last part?
> **Client:** Of course. I was asking when the API will be ready.
> **You:** Got it. It should be ready by the end of this week.

---

## 🔑 Key phrases to reuse

- "Can you hear me okay?" / "Am I coming through clearly?"
- "Sorry, you're breaking up — could you say that again?"
- "I think you're on mute."
- "Let me share my screen."
- "Give me a second." / "Bear with me for a moment."
- "Sorry, could you repeat that? I didn't catch it."

---

## 🗣️ Say it out loud (drill)

Practise these out loud, imagining a real call:
1. Start a call: "Hi everyone, can you hear me okay?"
2. Handle a glitch: "Sorry, you broke up — could you repeat that?"
3. Buy time: "Give me a second, I'll share my screen."

Record yourself. Do your phrases sound polite and relaxed?

---

## ⚠️ Watch out (common Hindi-speaker mistake)

- ❌ "I am not able to listen you."
- ✅ "I **can't hear** you."
- **Why:** **Hear** = sound reaching your ears (passive). **Listen** = paying attention
  on purpose. On a bad call, you can't *hear*. Also use "can't hear **you**" (no "to").

- ❌ "Say again please?" *(sounds abrupt)*
- ✅ "Sorry, **could you** repeat that?"
- **Why:** "Could you…" is more polite and natural on professional calls.
