# B1 · Lesson 1 — Daily Standup / Scrum Update

## 🎯 Goal
Give a clear, confident standup update in 3 sentences — the single most common
speaking task for a developer.

---

## 📖 The standup formula

A good standup answer has **3 parts**:
1. **Yesterday** — what you did.
2. **Today** — what you'll do.
3. **Blockers** — anything stopping you (or "no blockers").

| English | Meaning (Hindi hint) | Example |
|---------|----------------------|---------|
| Yesterday I… | कल मैंने… | "Yesterday I finished the login API." |
| Today I'll… / I'm going to… | आज मैं… | "Today I'll start on the payment module." |
| I'm blocked on… | मैं … पर अटका हूँ | "I'm blocked on the database access." |
| No blockers | कोई रुकावट नहीं | "No blockers from my side." |
| On track | सही रास्ते पर | "The feature is on track for Friday." |

---

## 💬 Dialogue — A standup round

> **Scrum Master:** Sanjay, you're up. What's your update?
> **You:** Sure. Yesterday I completed the user authentication API and wrote the unit
> tests. Today I'll start integrating it with the frontend. I don't have any blockers
> right now.
> **Scrum Master:** Great. Is it on track for the sprint?
> **You:** Yes, it's on track. I should finish by tomorrow.

### If you ARE blocked:
> **You:** Yesterday I was working on the payment service, but I'm blocked — I don't
> have access to the test database yet. Could someone help me with that after the call?

---

## 🔑 Key phrases to reuse

- "Yesterday I **finished / completed / worked on** …"
- "Today I'll **start / continue / focus on** …"
- "I'm **blocked on** … / I don't have any blockers."
- "It's **on track** for …"
- "Could someone help me with … after the call?"

---

## 🗣️ Say it out loud (drill)

Give YOUR real standup update right now, out loud, in exactly 3 sentences:
1. "Yesterday I __________."
2. "Today I'll __________."
3. "I'm blocked on __________." *(or)* "No blockers."

Do it again, faster and smoother. Record the second try.

---

## ⚠️ Watch out (common Hindi-speaker mistake)

- ❌ "Yesterday I am completing the API."
- ✅ "Yesterday I **completed** the API."
- **Why:** "Yesterday" = past, so use **past tense** (completed), not continuous.

- ❌ "Today I will doing frontend."
- ✅ "Today I **will do** / I'm **going to do** the frontend."
- **Why:** After "will", use the base verb ("will do"), not "-ing".

- ❌ "I am stuck since 2 days."
- ✅ "I've been stuck **for** 2 days." / "I've been stuck **since** Monday."
- **Why:** **for** + duration (2 days), **since** + starting point (Monday).
