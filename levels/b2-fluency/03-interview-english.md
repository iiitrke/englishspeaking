# B2 · Lesson 3 — Technical Interview English

## 🎯 Goal
Answer common interview questions and talk about your projects clearly — so your
English never hides your real skills.

---

## 📖 The STAR structure (for experience questions)

For "Tell me about a time…" questions, use **STAR**:
- **S**ituation — set the scene
- **T**ask — what you had to do
- **A**ction — what *you* did
- **R**esult — the outcome

| English | Example |
|---------|---------|
| The situation was… | "The situation was, our API was timing out under load." |
| My task was to… | "My task was to improve the response time." |
| So I… | "So I added caching and optimized the queries." |
| As a result… | "As a result, response time dropped by 60%." |

---

## 💬 Common questions + strong answers

**"Tell me about yourself."**
> I'm a Java developer with around four years of experience. I mostly work with Spring
> Boot, building REST APIs and microservices. I enjoy backend work — especially designing
> clean, testable code. Recently I've been focusing on improving system performance.

**"Tell me about a challenging project."**
> Sure. The situation was, we had an order service that was very slow under heavy load.
> My task was to find and fix the bottleneck. So I profiled the application, found some
> N+1 query problems, and added proper indexing and caching. As a result, we cut the
> average response time roughly in half.

**"Why do you want this role?"**
> I'm looking for a role where I can work on larger-scale systems and keep growing. From
> what I've read, your team works on exactly that kind of problem, and I'd love to contribute.

---

## 🔑 Key phrases to reuse

- "I have around **[X]** years of experience in **[tech]**."
- "I'm particularly strong in **[area]**."
- "The situation was… My task was… So I… As a result…"
- "I'm looking for a role where I can **[goal]**."
- "That's a great question — let me think for a second." *(buys time, totally fine!)*

---

## 🗣️ Say it out loud (drill)

Record full answers (1–2 minutes each):
1. "Tell me about yourself."
2. "Tell me about a challenging bug or project." (Use STAR.)
3. "Why should we hire you?"

Listen back. Were your answers structured, or did you ramble? Redo #2 using clear STAR.

---

## ⚠️ Watch out (common Hindi-speaker mistake)

- ❌ "I am working in this company since 4 years."
- ✅ "I **have been working** at this company **for** 4 years."
- **Why:** Ongoing-since-past = present perfect continuous ("have been working").
  Use "**for** 4 years" (duration) or "**since** 2022" (start point). And "**at** a company".

- ❌ "I did my graduation in 2020."
- ✅ "I **graduated** in 2020." / "I **finished my degree** in 2020."
- **Why:** "Did my graduation" is Indian English; "graduated" is standard.

- 💡 **Tip:** Silence is okay. "Let me think for a moment" sounds *confident*, not weak.
