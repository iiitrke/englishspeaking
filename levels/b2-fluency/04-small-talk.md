# B2 · Lesson 4 — Small Talk with Team & Clients

## 🎯 Goal
Make easy small talk before meetings and with clients. Small talk builds relationships —
and it's low-pressure practice that makes you comfortable speaking.

---

## 📖 Phrases

| English | Meaning (Hindi hint) | Example |
|---------|----------------------|---------|
| How's it going? | कैसा चल रहा है? | "Hey, how's it going?" |
| How was your weekend? | वीकेंड कैसा रहा? | "How was your weekend?" |
| Keeping busy? | व्यस्त हैं? | "Pretty busy with the release — you?" |
| Any plans for…? | … के लिए कोई प्लान? | "Any plans for the long weekend?" |
| Same here | मेरा भी वही हाल | "Tired? Same here!" |
| Anyway, shall we start? | खैर, शुरू करें? | "Anyway, shall we get started?" |

---

## 💬 Dialogue — Before a meeting

> **Client:** Hey Sanjay, how's it going?
> **You:** Pretty good, thanks! A bit busy with the release, but things are on track.
> How about you?
> **Client:** Same here. How was your weekend?
> **You:** It was relaxing. I mostly caught up on some rest. How about you — any plans?
> **Client:** Just family time. Anyway, shall we get started?
> **You:** Sure, let's dive in.

---

## 🔑 Key phrases to reuse

- **Openers:** "How's it going?" / "How are things?" / "How was your weekend?"
- **Replies:** "Pretty good, thanks — you?" / "Can't complain!" / "Busy, but good."
- **Keep it going:** "Oh nice!" / "That sounds fun." / "Same here."
- **Move to business:** "Anyway, shall we get started?" / "Okay, let's dive in."

---

## 🗣️ Say it out loud (drill)

Practise a 20-second small-talk exchange out loud (play both roles):
1. Greet and ask how they are.
2. Answer when they ask you back.
3. Smoothly move to business: "Anyway, shall we get started?"

---

## ⚠️ Watch out (common Hindi-speaker mistake)

- ❌ "I am fine. And you?" *(okay, but very textbook/stiff)*
- ✅ "Pretty good, thanks — how about you?" / "Can't complain, you?"
- **Why:** Native small talk is relaxed and casual. "I am fine, and you?" sounds like a
  school textbook. Loosen up.

- ❌ "What is your good name?"
- ✅ "What's your name?" / "Sorry, I didn't catch your name."
- **Why:** "Good name" is a direct translation of "शुभ नाम" — charming, but not standard English.
