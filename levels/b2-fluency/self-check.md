# ✅ B2 Self-Check

Speak **out loud**, **record**, self-correct. These are close to real career situations.

## Speaking prompts (1–2 minutes each)

1. Walk someone through a real class/method you wrote. Explain the idea, one decision, and a trade-off.
2. A teammate suggests a design you disagree with. Disagree politely and propose an alternative.
3. Interview: "Tell me about a challenging project." Use STAR.
4. Make 20 seconds of small talk with a client, then move to business.

## Check yourself

- [ ] Did I use simple verbs ("handles", "processes") not "doing the handling of"?
- [ ] Did I say "I **made** changes" / "I **have made**" (not "I have did")?
- [ ] Did I disagree **politely** ("I see your point, but…")?
- [ ] Did I say "I've been working **for** 4 years" / "**since** 2022"?
- [ ] Did I say "In my opinion" (not "According to me")?
- [ ] Did my interview answer have a clear structure (STAR)?

## How to self-correct

1. Record → listen → note 1–2 recurring mistakes.
2. Keep a small "my common mistakes" list. Review it before you speak.
3. Re-record your weakest prompt once a week and compare.

🎉 **If you can do all four prompts comfortably, your English is no longer a career barrier.**
Keep the daily routine going — fluency is a habit, not a finish line.
