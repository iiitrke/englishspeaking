# 🗣️ English Speaking Course for Developers

A practical, **speaking-first** English course — built especially for
**Hindi-speaking software developers** who are strong at coding but want to break the
**communication barrier** in their career.

> You already crossed the beginner level. Now the goal is **fluent, confident speaking**
> in standups, code reviews, interviews, and client calls.

---

## 👤 Who this is for

- You can read/write English okay, but **speaking makes you nervous**.
- You hesitate in **standups, meetings, and interviews**.
- You can explain code to a teammate in Hindi easily, but it feels hard in English.
- You want **real, usable phrases** — not boring grammar theory.

## 🎯 What you'll be able to do

- Give a clear daily **standup update** without freezing.
- **Explain your Spring Boot / Java code** in a code review.
- Handle **phone and video calls** (and politely ask people to repeat).
- Answer **technical interview** questions and talk about your projects.
- Make **small talk** with your team and clients.

## 🔑 The one rule that matters most

> **Speak OUT LOUD every day.** Reading silently will not make you fluent.
> Even 15 minutes of speaking aloud beats 2 hours of silent reading.
