# ▶️ Start Here — How to Use This Course

Welcome, Sanjay! 👋 You write good Java and Spring Boot code. English is just one
more skill — and like coding, you get good at it by **practising daily**, not by
reading theory.

---

## 🧠 The mindset (read this once)

1. **Mistakes are fine.** Nobody speaks perfectly. People understand you even with
   small errors. Fear of mistakes is the #1 reason developers stay silent in meetings.
2. **Speaking ≠ reading.** You must move your mouth and make sound. Thinking the
   sentence in your head is not practice.
3. **Fluency before perfection.** First goal: keep talking without stopping. Grammar
   gets better automatically with practice.
4. **Think in English, don't translate.** When you translate from Hindi word-by-word,
   you speak slowly and make "Indian English" mistakes. We'll fix this.

---

## ⏱️ Your daily routine (20–30 minutes)

Do this every day. Treat it like a daily commit. 💚

| Time | Activity | How |
|------|----------|-----|
| 5 min | **Warm-up** | Read one lesson's dialogue **out loud**, twice. |
| 10 min | **Shadowing** | Pick an English tech video (see below). Play a sentence, pause, repeat it exactly — copy the rhythm. |
| 5 min | **Speak freely** | Answer one [daily speaking prompt](practice/daily-speaking-prompts.md) out loud for 1–2 minutes. **Record yourself** on your phone. |
| 5 min | **Self-correct** | Listen to your recording. Note 1–2 mistakes. Say those sentences again, correctly. |

Then tick the day in your **[30-day tracker](practice/30-day-tracker.md)**.

## 🗺️ Suggested path

1. Read **A2 Foundation** (2 lessons) — rebuild confidence. ~3 days.
2. Move to **B1 Conversation** — standups, calls, asking for help. ~2 weeks.
3. Then **B2 Fluency** — explaining code, opinions, interviews. ~2 weeks.
4. Keep the **reference** pages open while you practise.
5. Do the **self-check** at the end of each level before moving on.

You do **not** have to finish a level before starting daily speaking practice.
Start speaking from Day 1.

---

## 📺 Free tools for listening & speaking practice

- **Shadowing material:** YouTube tech talks (e.g. conference talks on Java/Spring),
  slowed to 0.75x speed if needed.
- **Recording:** Your phone's voice recorder — compare your voice to the model.
- **Speaking partner:** If you have no partner, speak to yourself in the mirror, or
  explain your code out loud in English as if teaching a junior.
- **Captions on:** Watch English content with English subtitles (not Hindi) to connect
  sound ↔ spelling.

---

## 💡 Developer tip

You already explain logic every day. Each time you solve a bug or review a PR,
**say the explanation out loud in English**, as if in a standup:
> "So the issue was a null pointer in the service layer. I added a null check and a
> unit test to cover it."

That is real, free, daily practice using work you're already doing.
