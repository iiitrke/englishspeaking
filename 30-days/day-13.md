# Day 13 — Everyday Situations

🎯 **Today's goal:** Handle shopping, restaurant, and travel in English.

## 📐 Grammar (short)
Be polite: "Could I **get** a coffee, please?" (NOT "Give me one coffee"). Drop the "…, no?" tag.

## 🗣️ Speak out loud (play both roles)
> **You:** "Excuse me, do you have this in a larger size?" … "Could I try it?" … "I'll take it. Can I pay by card?"
> **Restaurant:** "What would you recommend?" … "I'll have that, please." … "Could we get the bill?"

Full lesson: [B1 · Everyday situations](../levels/b1-conversation/04-everyday-situations.md)

## ✍️ Exercise (say out loud, then check)
1. ❌ "Give me one coffee." → ✅ ___
2. ❌ "You are coming, no?" → ✅ ___
3. "What would you ___ ?" (recommend)

**Answers:** 1. "Could I **get** a coffee, please?" · 2. "You're coming, **right**?" · 3. **recommend**

## 🎯 Daily mission
Role-play ordering a meal out loud (both roles). Then do a shopping scene.
