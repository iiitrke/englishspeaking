# Day 1 — Introduce Yourself

🎯 **Today's goal:** Say a confident 30-second self-introduction.

## 📐 Grammar (short)
"I **am a** developer." — don't drop the **a**. And say "**I'm** Sanjay", NOT "Myself Sanjay".

## 🗣️ Speak out loud
Say this, then say it again with YOUR details. Record it.
> "Hi, I'm Sanjay. I'm a Java developer. I have four years of experience, mostly with
> Spring Boot. Right now I'm working on REST APIs. Nice to meet you."

Full lesson: [A2 · Greetings & introductions](../levels/a2-foundation/01-greetings-introductions.md)

## ✍️ Exercise (say out loud, then check)
Fill the blank:
1. "I'm ___ backend developer."
2. "I ___ five years of experience." (have / am having)
3. "___ Sanjay." (Myself / I'm)

**Answers:** 1. **a** · 2. **have** · 3. **I'm**

## 🎯 Daily mission
Introduce yourself out loud **3 times** in the mirror. Save the recording — you'll compare it on Day 30.
