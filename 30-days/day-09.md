# Day 9 — Past Simple

🎯 **Today's goal:** Talk about finished actions correctly.

## 📐 Grammar (short)
- Regular: add **-ed** (fixed, deployed). Irregular: did, made, wrote, went.
- Negatives/questions use **did** + base: "I **didn't** test it", "**Did** you push?".

## 🗣️ Speak out loud
> "Yesterday I fixed a bug, wrote the tests, and pushed my changes. I didn't deploy it yet.
> Did you review my PR?"

Full lesson: [Grammar · Past simple](../grammar/03-past-simple.md)

## ✍️ Exercise (say out loud, then check)
1. Yesterday I ___ (write) the docs.
2. We ___ (not / finish) on time.
3. ___ you ___ (push) the code?
4. He ___ (make) a mistake.

**Answers:** 1. **wrote** · 2. **didn't finish** · 3. **Did … push** · 4. **made**

## 🎯 Daily mission
Describe what you did yesterday at work in 5 past-tense sentences. Record it.
