# Day 6 — Review & Record (Week 1)

🎯 **Today's goal:** Put Week 1 together and hear your progress.

## 📐 Grammar (short recap)
- Articles: a / an / the. · Habits = present simple. · State verbs = no -ing.

## 🗣️ Speak out loud (record all three)
1. 30-second self-introduction.
2. Your daily routine (first / then / after that / finally).
3. What you build at work (1 minute).

## ✍️ Exercise (fix the mistake, say the correct version out loud)
1. ❌ "Myself Sanjay." → ✅ ___
2. ❌ "I am having 4 years experience." → ✅ ___
3. ❌ "Daily I am going to office." → ✅ ___

**Answers:** 1. "I'm Sanjay." · 2. "I **have** 4 years **of** experience." · 3. "I **go** to the office **every day**."

## 🎯 Daily mission
Listen to today's recordings. Write down ONE mistake you keep making. That's your focus for next week.
