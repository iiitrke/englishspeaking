# Day 8 — Daily Standup

🎯 **Today's goal:** Give a 3-sentence standup update smoothly.

## 📐 Grammar (short)
"Yesterday" = past tense: "Yesterday I **completed**…" (NOT "I am completing").

## 🗣️ Speak out loud (the standup formula)
> "Yesterday I finished the login API. Today I'll start the frontend integration.
> No blockers from my side."

Full lesson: [B1 · Standup](../levels/b1-conversation/01-standup.md)

## ✍️ Exercise (say out loud, then check)
1. "Yesterday I ___ (complete) the API."
2. "Today I'll ___ (do / doing) the tests."
3. "I've been stuck ___ (since / for) two days."

**Answers:** 1. **completed** · 2. **do** · 3. **for**

## 🎯 Daily mission
Give your REAL standup update out loud in exactly 3 sentences (yesterday / today / blockers). Record it.
