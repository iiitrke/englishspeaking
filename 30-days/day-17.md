# Day 17 — Explaining Your Code

🎯 **Today's goal:** Walk someone through your code in a review.

## 📐 Grammar (short)
Simple verbs: "This **handles** retries." Say "I **made** changes" (NOT "I have did").

## 🗣️ Speak out loud
> "Let me walk you through it. The idea here is to keep the logic out of the controller.
> I decided to use an interface because it's easier to test. The trade-off is a bit more code."

Full lesson: [B2 · Explaining your code](../levels/b2-fluency/01-explaining-code.md)

## ✍️ Exercise (say out loud, then check)
1. ❌ "This code is doing the handling of the request." → ✅ ___
2. ❌ "I have did the changes." → ✅ ___
3. "The ___ is simplicity versus speed." (trade-off)

**Answers:** 1. "This code **handles** the request." · 2. "I **made** the changes." · 3. **trade-off**

## 🎯 Daily mission
Pick a REAL class/method you wrote. Explain it out loud: the idea, one decision, one trade-off. Record it.
