# Day 7 — Free Speaking Day

🎯 **Today's goal:** Just talk. No rules. Build the habit of speaking without fear.

## 📐 Grammar (short)
None today — fluency over perfection. Keep talking even if you make mistakes.

## 🗣️ Speak out loud (pick any 2, speak 2 min each)
- What did you do this week?
- Describe your favourite technology and why you like it.
- Explain the last bug you fixed.
- What do you want to achieve in your career?

More prompts: [Daily speaking prompts](../practice/daily-speaking-prompts.md)

## ✍️ Exercise
No written exercise today. Instead: **talk for 2 full minutes without stopping** on any
topic above. If you get stuck, say "um… let me think" and continue — that's normal.

## 🎯 Daily mission
Record a 2-minute free talk. Don't judge it. Just notice: could you keep going longer than Day 1? 🎉 You finished Week 1!
