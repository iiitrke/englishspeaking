# Day 12 — Asking for Help

🎯 **Today's goal:** Ask for help and clarification politely, without hesitation.

## 📐 Grammar (short)
"Explain this **to me**" (NOT "explain me this"). Say "I have a **question**" (NOT "a doubt").

## 🗣️ Speak out loud
> "Hey, do you have a minute? I'm a bit stuck on the deployment. Could you point me in
> the right direction? … Just to confirm, you mean the config file, right?"

Full lesson: [B1 · Asking for help](../levels/b1-conversation/03-asking-for-help.md)

## ✍️ Exercise (say out loud, then check)
1. ❌ "Can you explain me this?" → ✅ ___
2. ❌ "I have a doubt." → ✅ ___
3. "Just to ___ , you mean X, right?"

## 🎯 Daily mission
Think of a real thing you're stuck on. Out loud: ask for help in one polite sentence, then confirm the answer.
