# Day 22 — Prepositions (in / on / at / to)

🎯 **Today's goal:** Fix the small words that trip up Hindi speakers.

## 📐 Grammar (short)
- **at** + time (at 10) · **on** + day (on Monday) · **in** + month/year (in 2022).
- explain **to** · depend **on** · work **on** · good **at**.

## 🗣️ Speak out loud
> "The standup is **at** 10 **on** Monday. We shipped it **in** 2023. I'm working **on**
> the API. It depends **on** the load. I'm good **at** debugging."

Full lesson: [Grammar · Prepositions](../grammar/06-prepositions.md)
