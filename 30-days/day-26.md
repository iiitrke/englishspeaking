# Day 26 — Strengths & Weaknesses

🎯 **Today's goal:** Answer "What are your strengths and weaknesses?" honestly and well.

## 📐 Grammar (short)
Use present perfect for growth: "I've been **working on** improving my communication."

## 🗣️ Speak out loud (model)
> "My main strength is backend design — I write clean, testable code. A weakness I've been
> working on is public speaking; that's actually why I'm practising my English every day,
> and I've already improved a lot."

## ✍️ Exercise (say out loud, then check)
1. "My main ___ is problem-solving."
2. "A ___ I've been working on is time estimation."
3. ❌ "I am working on it since months." → ✅ ___

<details>
<summary>✅ Show answers</summary>

1. **strength** · 2. **weakness** · 3. "I've been working on it **for** months."

</details>

## 🎯 Daily mission
Record your real answer: 1 strength (with proof) + 1 weakness (with how you're improving it).
