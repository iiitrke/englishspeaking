# Day 16 — Word Stress

🎯 **Today's goal:** Stress the right syllable so people stop saying "sorry?".

## 📐 Pronunciation (short)
English hits ONE syllable harder. Wrong stress = hard to understand. (CAPS = stressed.)

## 🗣️ Speak out loud (clap on the CAPS syllable)
> de-**VEL**-op · de-**VEL**-op-ment · im-**POR**-tant · a-**VAIL**-a-ble · **CAT**-e-go-ry

Full lesson: [Pronunciation guide](../reference/pronunciation-guide.md)

## ✍️ Exercise (mark & say the stressed syllable)
1. develop → de-___-op
2. important → im-___-tant
3. available → a-___-a-ble
