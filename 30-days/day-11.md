# Day 11 — Present Perfect + since / for

🎯 **Today's goal:** Say how long you've done something, the right way.

## 📐 Grammar (short)
- have/has + past participle: "I **have worked** here…"
- **for** + duration (for 3 years). **since** + starting point (since 2022).

## 🗣️ Speak out loud
> "I've worked with Java **for** five years. I've been on this team **since** January.
> I've used Spring Boot before."

Full lesson: [Grammar · Present perfect & since/for](../grammar/04-present-perfect-since-for.md)

## ✍️ Exercise (say out loud, then check)
1. I ___ (work) here ___ three years.
2. She ___ (be) here ___ January.
3. I ___ (use) Docker before.

**Answers:** 1. **have worked … for** · 2. **has been … since** · 3. **have used**

Say 4 sentences about your experience using *for* and *since* correctly. Record and check.
