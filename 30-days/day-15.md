# Day 15 — Sounds: v / w and th

🎯 **Today's goal:** Say v/w and th clearly so people understand you.

## 📐 Pronunciation (short)
- **v** = teeth touch lip, buzz: **v**ery, de**v**eloper.
- **w** = round lips, no teeth: **w**ork, **w**ater.
- **th** = tongue between teeth: **th**ink, **th**is (NOT "tink / dis").

## 🗣️ Speak out loud (repeat 5 times each)
> "**v**ery / **w**ary", "**v**est / **w**est", "**v**ine / **w**ine"
> "**Th**ink **th**ree **th**ings about **th**is."

Full lesson: [Pronunciation guide](../reference/pronunciation-guide.md)

## ✍️ Exercise (say aloud, feel your mouth)
1. "I'm a **v**ery good de**v**eloper." (teeth on lip for v)
2. "**W**e **w**ill **w**ork on it." (round lips for w)
3. "**Th**ank you for **th**e code re**v**iew." (th + v)

Record yourself saying 3 v/w pairs + "think three things". Compare to Google's 🔊 (type a word, tap the speaker).
