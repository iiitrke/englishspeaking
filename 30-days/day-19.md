# Day 19 — Giving Opinions

🎯 **Today's goal:** Share your opinion in a technical discussion.

## 📐 Grammar (short)
Say "**In my opinion**…" (NOT "According to me"). "According to" is for other sources ("according to the docs").

## 🗣️ Speak out loud
> "In my opinion, we should refactor this first. The way I see it, it'll save time later.
> I'd suggest we add tests before merging."

Full lesson: [B2 · Giving opinions](../levels/b2-fluency/02-giving-opinions.md)
