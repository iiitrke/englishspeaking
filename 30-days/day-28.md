# Day 28 — Mock Interview

🎯 **Today's goal:** Put it all together in a full mock interview.

## 📐 Grammar (short)
Buy time confidently: "That's a good question — let me think for a second." Silence is fine.

## 🗣️ Speak out loud (record ALL — like a real interview)
1. "Tell me about yourself."
2. "Tell me about a challenging project." (STAR)
3. "What are your strengths and weaknesses?"
4. "Why do you want to join us?"
5. "Where do you see yourself in 3 years?"

Full lesson: [B2 · Interview English](../levels/b2-fluency/03-interview-english.md)

## ✍️ Exercise (self-review after recording)
- [ ] Did I structure the project answer with STAR?
- [ ] Did I use "for/since" correctly?
- [ ] Did I pause confidently instead of panicking?

## 🎯 Daily mission
Do the full 5-question mock interview in one recording. Listen back and note 2 wins + 1 fix.
