# Day 25 — Modals & Polite Requests

🎯 **Today's goal:** Make polite requests and give advice.

## 📐 Grammar (short)
Modal + **base verb**: "**Could** you **review** this?" (NOT "could to review"). should = advice, must = rule.

## 🗣️ Speak out loud
> "Could you review my PR, please? We should add tests before merging. You must not push
> directly to main. I have to finish this today."

Full lesson: [Grammar · Modals](../grammar/07-modals.md)

## ✍️ Exercise (say out loud, then check)
1. "___ you help me, please?"
2. "We ___ write tests."
3. "You ___ never push to main."

<details>
<summary>✅ Show answers</summary>

1. **Could** · 2. **should** · 3. **must**

</details>

## 🎯 Daily mission
Turn 3 commands into polite requests out loud: "Give me the file" → "Could you send me the file, please?"
