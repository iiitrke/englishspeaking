# Day 24 — Project Story (STAR)

🎯 **Today's goal:** Tell a project/challenge story with a clear structure.

## 📐 Structure (short) — STAR
**S**ituation → **T**ask → **A**ction → **R**esult.

## 🗣️ Speak out loud (model)
> "The situation was, our order service was slow under load. My task was to fix the
> bottleneck. So I profiled it, found N+1 queries, and added indexing and caching.
> As a result, response time dropped by about half."

Full lesson: [B2 · Interview English](../levels/b2-fluency/03-interview-english.md)

## ✍️ Exercise (build your own STAR, say it out loud)
- The situation was ___
- My task was to ___
- So I ___
- As a result ___

(No single answer — fill with a real project and say it aloud.)

## 🎯 Daily mission
Record a 1–2 minute STAR story about a real bug or project you're proud of.
