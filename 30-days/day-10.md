# Day 10 — Phone & Video Calls

🎯 **Today's goal:** Handle calls and audio problems without panic.

## 📐 Grammar (short)
"I can't **hear** you" (sound reaching ears) — NOT "I can't listen you".

## 🗣️ Speak out loud
> "Hi, can you hear me okay? … Sorry, you're breaking up — could you repeat that?
> Let me share my screen. Give me a second."

Full lesson: [B1 · Phone & video calls](../levels/b1-conversation/02-phone-calls.md)

## ✍️ Exercise (say out loud, then check)
1. ❌ "I am not able to listen you." → ✅ ___
2. ❌ "Say again please?" → ✅ ___
3. "Let me ___ my screen."

<details>

1. "I **can't hear** you." · 2. "Sorry, **could you repeat that**?" · 3. **share**

## 🎯 Daily mission
Role-play joining a call out loud: greet, hit an audio glitch, ask them to repeat, share your screen.
