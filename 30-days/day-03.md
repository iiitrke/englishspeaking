# Day 3 — Your Daily Routine

🎯 **Today's goal:** Describe your workday using *first / then / after that / finally*.

## 📐 Grammar (short)
Habits = **present simple**: "I **check** emails", "I **go** to the office" — NOT "I am going".

## 🗣️ Speak out loud
Describe YOUR day in 5 sentences, out loud:
> "My day usually starts with a standup. First, I check my PRs. Then I write code.
> After that, I fix bugs. Finally, I update my tickets."

Full lesson: [A2 · Daily routine](../levels/a2-foundation/02-daily-routine.md)

## ✍️ Exercise (say out loud, then check)
Choose the right form:
1. I ___ (go / am going) to the office every day.
2. I usually ___ (start / am starting) at 10.
3. Right now I ___ (write / am writing) this exercise.

**Answers:** 1. **go** · 2. **start** · 3. **am writing**

## 🎯 Daily mission
Record your real daily routine (5+ sentences). Listen back: did you pause too much? Say it again, smoother.
