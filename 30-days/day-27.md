# Day 27 — Small Talk

🎯 **Today's goal:** Make easy small talk before meetings.

## 📐 Grammar (short)
Keep it relaxed: "Pretty good, thanks — you?" (NOT the stiff "I am fine, and you?"). Say "What's your name?" (NOT "good name").

## 🗣️ Speak out loud (play both roles)
> "Hey, how's it going?" — "Pretty good, thanks! Busy with the release. You?"
> "How was your weekend?" — "Relaxing, thanks. Anyway, shall we get started?"
