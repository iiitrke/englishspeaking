# Day 30 — 🎉 Final Self-Intro & Compare to Day 1

🎯 **Today's goal:** Record a polished 2-minute self-introduction and hear how far you've come.

## 📐 Grammar (short recap — your top fixes)
- "I **have** X years **of** experience" · "I'm Sanjay" (not "Myself") · past tense for "yesterday"
- "for/since" · "In my opinion" · "explain **to me**" · modals + base verb.

## 🗣️ Speak out loud (your final recording)
Give a 2-minute talk covering:
1. Who you are (name, role, experience).
2. What you build and enjoy.
3. A project you're proud of (STAR).
4. Where you want to go next.

## ✍️ Exercise — the big comparison
1. Play your **Day 1** recording.
2. Play your **Day 30** recording.
3. Write down: What improved? (fluency / confidence / fewer pauses / clearer sounds?)
