# Day 23 — "Tell Me About Yourself"

🎯 **Today's goal:** Nail the most common interview question.

## 📐 Grammar (short)
"I've been working **for** 4 years" / "**since** 2022". Say "I **graduated** in 2020" (NOT "did my graduation").

## 🗣️ Speak out loud (model answer)
> "I'm a Java developer with around four years of experience. I mostly work with Spring
> Boot, building REST APIs and microservices. I enjoy backend work, especially clean,
> testable code. Recently I've been focusing on performance."

Full lesson: [B2 · Interview English](../levels/b2-fluency/03-interview-english.md)

## ✍️ Exercise (say out loud, then check)
1. ❌ "I am working here since 4 years." → ✅ ___
2. ❌ "I did my graduation in 2020." → ✅ ___
3. "I'm particularly ___ in backend." (strong)

**Answers:** 1. "I **have been working** here **for** 4 years." · 2. "I **graduated** in 2020." · 3. **strong**

## 🎯 Daily mission
Record your own "Tell me about yourself" (60–90 sec). Keep it: role → experience → strengths → focus now.
