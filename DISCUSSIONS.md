# 💬 Discussions — Practise With Others

During your learning phase, use **GitHub Discussions** to host conversations, post
tutorials, ask questions, and get feedback. It's free and built into this repo.

---

## ✅ How to turn Discussions on (one-time)

1. Go to the repo on GitHub: `https://github.com/iiitrke/englishspeaking`
2. Click **Settings** (top menu).
3. Scroll to **Features**.
4. Tick ✅ **Discussions**.
5. A new **Discussions** tab appears at the top of the repo.

Direct link once enabled: <https://github.com/iiitrke/englishspeaking/discussions>

---

## 🗂️ Suggested categories

When you set up Discussions, GitHub lets you create categories. Suggested ones:

| Category | Use it for |
|----------|-----------|
| 📣 **Announcements** | New lessons, course updates (maintainer posts). |
| 🙋 **Q&A** | "How do I say X in English?" — questions with a best answer. |
| 🗣️ **Speaking practice** | Post a link to your recording and ask for feedback. |
| 📝 **Tutorials** | Share a mini-lesson or tip you learned. |
| 💡 **Ideas** | Suggest new topics or situations to cover. |
| 💬 **General** | Introductions, motivation, anything else. |

---

## ✍️ How to post a good practice thread

**Title:** short and clear — e.g. *"Feedback on my 1-min self-introduction"*

**Body template:**
```
## What I practised
A 60-second self-introduction (B2 Lesson about interviews).

## My recording / text
[link to audio]  — or paste what you said as text.

## What I want feedback on
- Did my pronunciation sound clear?
- Was my grammar correct?
- Did I sound confident?
```

---

## 🤝 Etiquette

- Be kind and encouraging — everyone here is learning.
- Correct mistakes gently and explain the *why*.
- Reply in **English** where you can — it's extra practice!
- Mark the most helpful reply as the **answer** in Q&A threads.

---

## 📺 Hosting tutorials on the web

Your written tutorials are already a free website via **GitHub Pages** (see the main
[README](README.md#-hosting-this-course-on-the-web)). For **video/audio** tutorials:

- Record a short screen+voice tutorial, upload to YouTube (unlisted is fine), and link it
  in a **Tutorials** discussion.
- Or drag an audio/video file directly into a Discussion comment — GitHub hosts it for you.

Happy practising! 🚀
