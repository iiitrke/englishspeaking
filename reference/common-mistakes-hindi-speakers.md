# 📖 Common Mistakes Hindi Speakers Make

These errors come from translating directly from Hindi (**L1 interference**). Fix these
few and your English will instantly sound much more natural. Review this page monthly.

---

## 1. Articles (a / an / the)

Hindi has no articles, so they often get dropped or misused.

- ❌ "I am developer." → ✅ "I am **a** developer."
- ❌ "Open the browser and go to Google." (if any browser) → ✅ "Open **a** browser…"
- ❌ "I work in IT industry." → ✅ "I work in **the** IT industry."
- **Rule of thumb:** `a/an` = one of many (any one). `the` = a specific one both people know.

## 2. Present continuous overused

Hindi often uses "-ing"-like forms where English uses simple present.

- ❌ "I am having a car." → ✅ "I **have** a car." (possession = no -ing)
- ❌ "I am knowing the answer." → ✅ "I **know** the answer." (state verbs: know, like, want, need)
- ❌ "Daily I am going to office." → ✅ "I **go** to the office every day." (habit = simple present)

## 3. "Since" vs "for"

- **since** + a *starting point*: "since Monday", "since 2022", "since morning".
- **for** + a *duration*: "for 3 days", "for 2 hours", "for a long time".
- ❌ "I am working here since 3 years." → ✅ "I **have been working** here **for** 3 years."

## 4. Prepositions

- ❌ "Discuss **about** the plan." → ✅ "Discuss the plan." (no "about")
- ❌ "Explain **me** this." → ✅ "Explain this **to me**."
- ❌ "I reached **to** office." → ✅ "I reached the office." / "I got **to** the office."
- ❌ "Married **with**." → ✅ "Married **to**."

## 5. Direct translation (word-for-word from Hindi)

- ❌ "Myself Sanjay." → ✅ "I'm Sanjay."
- ❌ "What is your good name?" → ✅ "What's your name?"
- ❌ "According to me…" → ✅ "In my opinion…"
- ❌ "I have a doubt." → ✅ "I have a question."
- ❌ "Do the needful." → ✅ "Could you please handle this / look into this?"
- ❌ "Please revert back." → ✅ "Please reply / get back to me." ("revert" already means "reply here"; "revert back" is redundant.)
- ❌ "Prepone the meeting." → ✅ "Can we move the meeting earlier?" ("Prepone" isn't standard English.)

## 6. Word order & tag questions

- ❌ "You are coming, no?" → ✅ "You're coming, right?" (drop the Hindi "ना?" habit)
- ❌ "Only I did it." (meaning *I'm the one who did it*) → ✅ "**I** did it." / "I did it **myself**."
- ❌ "Tell me what is the time." → ✅ "Tell me what **the time is**." (no question word order in reported speech)

## 7. "Do" vs "make"

- ❌ "I did a mistake." → ✅ "I **made** a mistake."
- ❌ "Do the changes." → ✅ "**Make** the changes."
- ❌ "I did my graduation." → ✅ "I **graduated**."

## 8. Countable / uncountable

- ❌ "I have many works." → ✅ "I have **a lot of work**." (work = uncountable)
- ❌ "Give me some informations." → ✅ "Give me some **information**." (no plural)
- ❌ "Many equipments." → ✅ "A lot of **equipment**."

---

### How to use this list
Don't memorize all at once. Pick **ONE** category per week, watch for it in your speaking,
and fix it. In 8 weeks you'll have cleaned up all the big ones. 💪
