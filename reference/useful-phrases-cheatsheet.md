# 📖 Useful Phrases Cheat Sheet

Keep this open while you work. Copy-paste-worthy phrases for a developer's day.

---

## 🧍 Standup
- "Yesterday I **finished / worked on** …"
- "Today I'll **start / continue** …"
- "I'm **blocked on** … / No blockers from my side."
- "It's **on track** for …"
- "I'll **pick that up** next."

## 📞 Meetings & calls
- "Can you hear me okay?"
- "Sorry, you're breaking up — could you repeat that?"
- "I think you're on mute."
- "Let me share my screen."
- "Give me a second." / "Bear with me."
- "Just to confirm, you mean …, right?"
- "Let's take that offline." (= discuss later, separately)
- "Can we **circle back** to this?" (= return to it later)
- "Anyway, shall we get started?"

## 🙋 Asking for help
- "Do you have a minute?"
- "I'm a bit stuck on … — could you help me out?"
- "Could you point me to the docs / the right person?"
- "That makes sense, thanks!"

## 💻 Explaining code
- "Let me walk you through it."
- "The idea here is to …"
- "I decided to … because …"
- "The trade-off is … versus …"
- "We could also …, but …"

## 🤔 Opinions & disagreeing (politely)
- "In my opinion …" / "The way I see it …"
- "I see your point, but …"
- "I'm not sure that'll work because …"
- "What if we …?" / "How about we …?"
- "That's fair." / "Good point." / "I'm on board."

## ✅ Agreeing & acknowledging
- "Sounds good." / "Works for me."
- "Got it." / "Makes sense."
- "Fair enough."
- "Let's go with that."

## ⏳ Buying time (totally professional!)
- "That's a good question — let me think for a second."
- "Good point, let me check and get back to you."
- "I'll look into it and update you."

## 🙏 Polite requests (instead of commands)
- "Could you please …?"
- "Would you mind …?"
- "When you get a chance, could you …?"

## 📝 Email / chat closings
- "Let me know if you have any questions."
- "Please get back to me when you can." (NOT "revert back")
- "Thanks in advance!"
- "Looking forward to your reply."

---

💡 **Tip:** Pick 5 phrases from here each week and *use them out loud* at work. That's how
they become automatic.
