# 📖 Pronunciation Guide for Hindi Speakers

You don't need a "perfect accent" — you need to be **clearly understood**. Fix these few
sounds and word-stress habits and people will understand you easily.

> Phonetic hints are written simply (no need to read IPA). IPA is shown as *optional extra*.

---

## 1. v vs w

Hindi often merges these. In English they're different.

- **v** — top teeth touch bottom lip, voice buzzes: **v**ery, **v**ote, de**v**eloper.
- **w** — round your lips like "oo", no teeth: **w**ater, **w**ork, **w**ould.
- Practice pair: "**v**est" vs "**w**est", "**v**ine" vs "**w**ine".

## 2. "th" sounds (two of them)

Hindi has no "th" as in English, so it often becomes "t" or "d".

- **Soft th** (voiceless) — tongue between teeth, no voice: **th**ink, **th**ree, ba**th**.
  (Not "tink/tree".)
- **Hard th** (voiced) — tongue between teeth, with voice: **th**is, **th**at, **th**ey, mo**th**er.
  (Not "dis/dat".)
- Practice: "**th**ink", "**th**ree **th**ings", "**th**is and **th**at".

## 3. Word stress (very important!)

English stresses ONE syllable strongly. Wrong stress is the #1 reason people say
"sorry?" to Indian speakers. CAPS = stressed syllable.

| Word | ✅ Correct stress | ❌ Common wrong |
|------|------------------|----------------|
| develop | de-**VEL**-op | DE-vel-op |
| development | de-**VEL**-op-ment | — |
| important | im-**POR**-tant | IM-por-tant |
| available | a-**VAIL**-a-ble | avail-A-ble |
| category | **CAT**-e-go-ry | cate-**GO**-ry |
| hierarchy | **HI**-er-ar-chy | hi-er-**AR**-chy |
| schedule | **SCHE**-dule ("SKED-jool" US / "SHED-yool" UK) | — |

## 4. Tech words people often mispronounce

| Word | Say it like | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|
| cache | "cash" | NOT "ca-shay". |
| query | "KWEER-ee" | the "qu" = "kw". |
| data | "DAY-ta" (US) / "DAH-ta" (UK) | both fine. |
| variable | "VAIR-ee-a-bul" | 4 syllables. |
| suite | "sweet" | NOT "soot". |
| route | "root" or "rowt" | both used. |
| GUI | "goo-ee" or "G-U-I" | both ok. |
| nginx | "engine-x" | — |
| Linux | "LIN-uks" | — |
| Java | "JAH-va" | — |

## 5. Silent letters

- **Wednesday** → "WENZ-day" (silent d).
- **comfortable** → "KUMF-ter-bul".
- **debt** → "det" (silent b).
- **often** → "OF-en" (t usually silent).
- **receipt** → "re-SEET" (silent p).

## 6. Final consonants — don't drop them

Hindi speakers sometimes soften word endings. Pronounce the final sound clearly:
- "wor**k**", "bugs" (ends in "z" sound), "fixe**d**", "deploye**d**".

---

## 🗣️ Daily 2-minute drill

1. Say 5 **v/w** pairs: vest/west, vine/wine, veil/wail, vary/wary, verse/worse.
2. Say "**th**ink **th**ree **th**ings about **th**is".
3. Say today's 3 tech words with correct stress (e.g. de-VEL-op-ment, im-POR-tant, a-VAIL-a-ble).

Record once a week. Compare to a model (type the word into Google and tap the 🔊 speaker icon).
