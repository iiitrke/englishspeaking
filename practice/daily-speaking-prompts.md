# 🏃 Daily Speaking Prompts

Pick ONE prompt a day. Speak for **1–2 minutes out loud** and **record** it. Then listen
and note one thing to improve. These are made for developers — so you practise exactly the
English you need at work.

> Tip: It's fine to repeat a prompt on another day — you'll sound better each time.

## Week 1 — About you & your work
1. Introduce yourself: name, role, experience, favourite technology.
2. Describe your current project. What are you building and why?
3. Walk through your typical workday from login to logout.
4. What part of coding do you enjoy most, and why?
5. Describe your tech stack (languages, frameworks, tools) in simple words.
6. Explain what Spring Boot is to a non-technical friend.
7. Talk about a technology you want to learn next.

## Week 2 — Explaining & describing
8. Explain the last bug you fixed. What was wrong and how did you fix it?
9. Describe a feature you're proud of. Why are you proud of it?
10. Explain the difference between two things (e.g. REST vs GraphQL, SQL vs NoSQL).
11. Walk someone through how a web request flows through your application.
12. Explain OOP (encapsulation, inheritance, polymorphism) in plain English.
13. Describe your git workflow (branches, pull requests, reviews).
14. Explain why writing tests matters, in your own words.

## Week 3 — Opinions & discussions
15. Should every method have a unit test? Give your opinion.
16. Microservices vs monolith — what do you prefer and why?
17. A teammate wants to skip code review to save time. Respond.
18. What makes code "clean"? Share your view.
19. Is it better to fix tech debt now or ship the feature? Argue your side.
20. Describe a time you disagreed with a teammate and how you handled it.
21. What's one engineering practice every team should follow?

## Week 4 — Career & interview
22. "Tell me about yourself." (interview answer)
23. "Tell me about a challenging project." (use STAR)
24. "What are your strengths and weaknesses?"
25. "Why do you want to change jobs / join this company?"
26. "Where do you see yourself in 3 years?"
27. Explain your biggest technical achievement so far.
28. Pitch yourself in 60 seconds to a hiring manager.

## Keep going
29. Teach a concept you know well as if to a junior developer.
30. Summarize something you read or watched this week (tech article/video).

---

### 🔁 How to get the most out of this
1. **Say it, don't just read it.** Move your mouth.
2. **Record every time.** Your phone is enough.
3. **Listen once for flow, once for mistakes.** Fix just one thing.
4. **Reuse phrases** from the [cheat sheet](../reference/useful-phrases-cheatsheet.md).
5. **No partner? No problem.** Talk to the mirror or explain to an imaginary junior dev.
