# Grammar 2 — Present Simple vs Continuous

## 🔹 The rule (short)
- **Present simple** (I work / he works) = habits & facts. Add **-s** for he/she/it.
- **Present continuous** (I am working) = happening right now / these days.
- **State verbs** (have, know, like, want, need, understand) → usually **no -ing**.

## 🔹 Examples
- "I **deploy** every Friday." (habit → simple)
- "I'm **debugging** this right now." (now → continuous)
- "I **have** a laptop." (NOT "I am having") — state verb
- "She **knows** the answer." (NOT "is knowing")

---

## ✍️ Practice (choose the correct form, say it out loud)

1. I ___ (check / am checking) my emails every morning.
2. Please wait, I ___ (fix / am fixing) the bug right now.
3. He ___ (have / has) five years of experience.
4. We usually ___ (deploy / are deploying) on Fridays.
5. I ___ (know / am knowing) the answer.
6. Right now the team ___ (works / is working) on the login feature.
7. She ___ (want / wants) a code review before merging.
8. I ___ (write / am writing) unit tests at the moment.

---

## ✅ Answer key
1. **check** 2. **am fixing** 3. **has** 4. **deploy** 5. **know** 6. **is working** 7. **wants** 8. **am writing**
