# Grammar 4 — Present Perfect & "since / for"

## 🔹 The rule (short)
- **Present perfect** = *have/has + past participle* (have **worked**, has **done**).
- Use it for: experience ("I **have used** Spring Boot") and actions that started in the
  past and continue now ("I **have worked** here for 3 years").
- **for** + duration (for 3 years, for 2 hours). **since** + starting point (since 2022, since Monday).

## 🔹 Examples
- "I **have worked** here **for** 3 years." (NOT "I work here since 3 years")
- "I **have been** at this company **since** 2022."
- "I **have used** Docker before." (experience)
- "She **has fixed** the bug." (done, result matters now)
