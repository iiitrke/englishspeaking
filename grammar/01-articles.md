# Grammar 1 — Articles: a / an / the

## 🔹 The rule (short)
- **a / an** = one of many (any one). Use **an** before a vowel sound (a, e, i, o, u).
- **the** = a specific one both people already know.
- Use **no article** for general plural or uncountable ideas ("I like coffee", "Developers write code").

## 🔹 Examples
- "I am **a** developer." (one of many)
- "I use **an** IDE." (vowel sound → an)
- "**The** bug is in the login service." (a specific, known bug)
- "I enjoy **coding**." (general idea → no article)
