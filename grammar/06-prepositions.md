# Grammar 6 — Prepositions: in / on / at / to

## 🔹 The rule (short)
**Time:**
- **at** + clock time (at 10, at noon).
- **on** + day/date (on Monday, on 5th June).
- **in** + month/year/long period (in June, in 2022, in the morning).

**Common verb + preposition (just memorize these):**
- explain something **to** someone · listen **to** · depend **on** · work **on** · good **at**.

## 🔹 Examples
- "The standup is **at** 10 **on** Monday."
- "We released it **in** 2023."
- "Can you explain this **to** me?" (NOT "explain me this")
- "I'm working **on** the login feature."
- "It depends **on** the server load."
