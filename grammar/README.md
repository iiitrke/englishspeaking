# 📐 Grammar — Short & Simple (with Practice)

Just the grammar you need for **speaking** — explained short, with a **separate practice
exercise** for each point. Do one grammar point a day. Say your answers **out loud**, then
check the answer key at the bottom of each page.

> Rule: 2 minutes reading the rule → 5 minutes practice out loud. That's it.

## 📚 Grammar lessons

| # | Topic | What it fixes |
|---|-------|---------------|
| 1 | [Articles: a / an / the](01-articles.md) | "I am developer" → "I am **a** developer" |
| 2 | [Present simple vs continuous](02-present-simple-vs-continuous.md) | "I am having a car" → "I **have** a car" |
| 3 | [Past simple](03-past-simple.md) | "Yesterday I am doing" → "Yesterday I **did**" |
| 4 | [Present perfect & "since / for"](04-present-perfect-since-for.md) | "I work here since 3 years" → "I **have worked** here **for** 3 years" |
| 5 | [Future: will / going to](05-future.md) | "Today I will doing" → "Today I **will do**" |
| 6 | [Prepositions: in / on / at / to](06-prepositions.md) | "Explain me this" → "Explain this **to me**" |
| 7 | [Modals: can / could / should / must](07-modals.md) | Polite requests & advice |
