# Grammar 3 — Past Simple

## 🔹 The rule (short)
- Finished action in the past (often with *yesterday, last week, in 2022*).
- Regular verbs: add **-ed** (fix → fixed, deploy → deployed).
- Irregular verbs: learn them (do → **did**, make → **made**, write → **wrote**, go → **went**).
- Negative/question: use **did** + base verb ("I **didn't** finish", "**Did** you test it?").

## 🔹 Examples
- "Yesterday I **fixed** the bug."
- "I **wrote** the unit tests last week."
- "I **didn't** deploy it yet." (NOT "didn't deployed")
- "**Did** you review the PR?"

---

## ✍️ Practice (put the verb in past simple, say out loud)

1. Yesterday I ___ (complete) the API.
2. Last sprint we ___ (release) version 2.0.
3. I ___ (write) the documentation this morning.
4. She ___ (fix) the production issue quickly.
5. We ___ (not / finish) the testing on time.
6. ___ you ___ (push) your changes?
7. He ___ (go) to the client meeting.
8. I ___ (make) a mistake in the config.

---

## ✅ Answer key
1. **completed** 2. **released** 3. **wrote** 4. **fixed** 5. **did not (didn't) finish** 6. **Did** you **push** 7. **went** 8. **made**
