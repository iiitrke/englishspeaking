# Advanced 7 — Articles & Countable/Uncountable (Deep Dive)

## 🔹 The rule (short)
- **Uncountable nouns** (no plural, no "a"): information, work, software, feedback, advice,
  research, progress, access, equipment, knowledge, code.
- Quantify them with: *some, much, a lot of, a piece of* — NOT *a / many / two*.
  "some **information**", "a lot of **work**", "a piece of **advice**".
- Countable nouns: a bug, two bugs, many servers, a few tickets.

## 🔹 Examples
- ❌ "I have many works." → ✅ "I have **a lot of work**."
- ❌ "Give me some informations." → ✅ "Give me some **information**."
- ❌ "He gave me a good feedback." → ✅ "He gave me **some good feedback**." / "a **piece** of feedback".
- ✅ "We made **a lot of progress**." · "I need **access** to the server."
