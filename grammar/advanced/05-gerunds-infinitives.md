# Advanced 5 — Gerunds vs Infinitives

## 🔹 The rule (short)
- **Gerund (-ing)** after: enjoy, avoid, finish, keep, consider, suggest, mind, and after prepositions.
  "I enjoy **coding**." "I'm good at **debugging**."
- **Infinitive (to + verb)** after: want, need, decide, plan, hope, agree, learn, would like.
  "I need **to deploy**." "We decided **to refactor**."
- Some verbs take either with little change: start, begin, continue, like.

## 🔹 Examples
- "I avoid **merging** on Fridays." (gerund)
- "I plan **to migrate** the DB." (infinitive)
- "She's interested in **learning** Go." (preposition → gerund)
- "We agreed **to split** the service." (infinitive)
