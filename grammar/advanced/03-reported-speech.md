# Advanced 3 — Reported (Indirect) Speech

## 🔹 The rule (short)
When you report what someone said, the tense usually "moves back" one step:
- present → past · past → past perfect · will → would · can → could.
- Pronouns and time words shift too (I → he, today → that day, tomorrow → the next day).

## 🔹 Examples
- Direct: "I **am** blocked." → Reported: He said he **was** blocked.
- Direct: "I **finished** it." → Reported: She said she **had finished** it.
- Direct: "I **will** deploy tomorrow." → He said he **would** deploy the next day.
- Direct: "**Can** you help?" → She asked **if I could** help.

## ✍️ Practice (rewrite as reported speech, say it out loud)

1. "I am testing the API." → He said he ___ the API.
2. "We released it yesterday." → They said they ___ it the day before.
3. "I will fix it tomorrow." → She said she ___ it the next day.
4. "I can reproduce the bug." → He said he ___ reproduce the bug.
5. "The build is failing." → She said the build ___.
6. "I have reviewed your PR." → He said he ___ my PR.
7. "I don't understand the ticket." → She said she ___ the ticket.
8. "Are you blocked?" → He asked ___ I ___ blocked.
9. "We must add tests." → They said they ___ add tests.
10. "I'm deploying now." → He said he ___ then.
